## Problem Statement

**Problem:** Fairness work in AI is too often bolted on at the end of development, when changes are costly and bias is already embedded. There is little accountability, no clear ownership, and no consistent way to document decisions.  

**Solution:** This playbook provides a **systematic, end‑to‑end framework** for embedding fairness into AI development – from team‑level practice, to organisation‑wide governance, to architecture‑specific strategies and regulatory compliance.  

**Context:** EquiHire is a fair AI‑based recruitment startup in the EU, serving B2B clients who require high levels of fairness and regulatory compliance across hiring workflows.  

**Audience:** Director‑level leaders and managers, product teams, data science and engineering teams, and legal / compliance stakeholders who need to use and coordinate these tools in their own domains.  

---

## How to Navigate This Playbook

You should read and apply the components **in this order**:

1. **Fair AI Scrum Toolkit (Component 1)**  
   Team‑level toolkit. Enhances user stories, acceptance criteria, Definition of Done, sprint backlogs, and ceremonies so that fairness is part of everyday agile practice.

2. **Organisational Integration Toolkit (Component 2)**  
   Organisation‑level governance. Defines roles, RACI, governance bodies, decision tiers, escalation paths, mandatory fairness gates, documentation templates, dashboards, and communication.

3. **Advanced Architecture Cookbook (Component 3)**  
   Architecture‑specific strategies. Provides recipes for LLMs, recommendation systems, and vision/multi‑modal systems, with loops for identifying fairness issues and selecting mitigation strategies.

4. **Regulatory Compliance Guide (Component 4)**  
   Legal alignment. Maps EU AI Act and GDPR into risk classification (incl. TRS), concrete compliance tasks, documentation templates (Model Card, DPIA, transparency notices), and audit trail design.

5. **Implementation Guide**  
   Explains how to roll out Components 1–4 in phases, including key decision points, resource needs, typical pitfalls, and how to scale from a single team to multiple products.

6. **Adaptability Guidelines**  
   Shows how to adapt the playbook across **domains** (e.g. healthcare, finance, education, criminal justice) and **problem types** (classification, regression, recommendation, ranking), with domain‑specific metrics and thresholds.

7. **Validation Framework**  
   Provides questions and metrics to assess whether your implementation of each component is working over time (process and outcome validation), and how to track improvement.

8. **Case Study**  
   End‑to‑end EquiHire example showing how three teams (resume screening, interviewing, job matching) apply all four components in sequence, and how outputs from one component become inputs to the next.

9. **Playbook Improvements**  
   Reflections and ideas for future iterations of the playbook (e.g. additional templates, visual workflows, deeper architecture‑governance alignment, expanded regulatory mapping).
