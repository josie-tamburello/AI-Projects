# Playbook Improvements  

Insights on how the playbook could be improved through future iterations:

---

## (1) Fair AI Scrum Toolkit  

### Resources  
- Add additional user story examples in the library.  

### Visualisation  
- Add a section on how teams can visualise fairness progress (sprint planning).  
- I could have produced a template for the team to use when visalising fairness progress alongside functional progress.  
- More emphasis could be placed on demonstration techniques.  

### Timing  
- Better implementing mapping of when bias issue appear in ceremony adaptation.  
- I have not quite captured this in the templates apart from adding date of filling out the template but something to improve on.  

### Integration of templates into infrastructure  
- Add suggestions on how template (e.g. daily standup questions template) could be integrated into systems, such as setting up a daily slack automation and feeding team responses into a spreadsheet for easier review.  

### Engagement  
- I could improve the templates to factor in stakeholder engagement at each stage of the process of ceremony to ensure that communication and mission remains strong.  

---

## (2) Organisational Integration Toolkit  

### Architecture-Governance Alignment  
- Introduce a mapping between AI architecture types (LLM, RecSys, Vision, Multi-modal) and required governance oversight, including mandatory review bodies, technical sign-offs and escalation thresholds per architecture.  

### System Risk Classification Layer  
- Before governance gates are applied, classify each AI system as Low, Medium, High or Critical risk.  
- Tie each level to required review bodies, approval authority and audit depth.  
- This makes governance scalable instead of uniform.  

---

## (3) Advanced Architecture Cookbook  

### Scope  
- Expand to additional architecture types (e.g. reinforcement learning systems, agent-based systems, search pipelines, hybrid models) and include clearer cross-architecture comparison guidance.  

### Templates  
- Provide practical, ready-to-use templates for each architecture, including fairness dashboard templates (exposure, drift, slice analysis), prompt and guardrail templates (LLMs), re-ranking logic templates (RecSys), saliency audit templates (Vision), and fusion calibration checklists (Multi-modal).  

### Detail  
- Add deeper implementation guidance for each strategy, including minimum viable fairness stacks, typical parameter ranges, starting configurations, and example industry benchmark thresholds (e.g. exposure gaps, drift tolerances, diversity targets).  

### Operationalisation  
- Include architecture diagrams showing where fairness interventions sit (objective layer, ranking layer, learning layer, monitoring layer) and how they integrate into existing pipelines.  

---
