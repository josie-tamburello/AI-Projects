# What to look for when buying table olives

1. **Check fermentation vs pasteurisation:** Slow fermentation can support the complex flavour (and live cultures) some buyers want. Heat-pasteurised jars, common in supermarkets, are shelf-stable but reduce the nutrients which are great for your microbiome. 

2. **Check natural vs fast (chemical) curing:** Natural curing may take 6–9 months; fast curing with lye / caustic soda can take 6–8 days when washed out correctly. Neither is automatically “bad,” but they are different products. Choose according to taste and values.

3. **Check whether olives are slit or whole:** Slitting speeds curing and changes texture; read the description if you care about firm bite vs softer style.

4. **Check pitted vs with pit:** Unpitted olives often need fewer preservatives, pitted ones usually rely on vinegar, salt, and sometimes more additives to keep.

5. **Check the ingredients:** prefer short lists like olives, water, salt plus oil / herbs / citrus for marinades. Avoid stuffed olives if you want a less processed product.
