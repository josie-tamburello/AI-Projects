# What to look for when buying tinned tomatoes

1. **Choose whole peeled plum tomatoes where possible:** they have the best texture and break down naturally in sauces; you can always crush them yourself, whereas chopped or diced tomatoes are more processed and often don’t soften as well.

2. **Check for additives and simple ingredients:** avoid tins with additives such as calcium chloride or added flavourings (e.g. herbs, garlic), and look for products with just tomatoes and possibly a small amount of salt.

3. **Look for quality certifications (e.g. D.O.P. San Marzano):** this indicates the tomatoes were grown and processed in a specific Italian region under strict standards and are generally associated with better flavour and quality.

4. **Understand the type of tomato used:** plum tomatoes are typically the best for richness and texture, cherry tomatoes are naturally sweeter, and chopped tomatoes are more convenient but less suitable for smooth sauces.

5. **Know when to use passata vs canned tomatoes:** passata is a smooth, thicker puree with seeds and skins removed, while canned tomatoes (whole or chopped) are less processed and better when you want more texture.
